65 — FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE
===========================

The website should **not feel like a website made for a hackathon.**

It should feel like:

> **the public-facing technical portal of an enterprise security product that happens to be being presented at SIH.**

That distinction is huge.

The deck says:

**"Here's our idea."**

The website should say:

**"Here's the system we've thought through."**

And the evidence page says:

**"Here's why you should believe us."**
